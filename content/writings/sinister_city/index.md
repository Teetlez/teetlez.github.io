{% <poem> %}

**beep**... **beep**... **beep**... _click_

It's always the same. Every day.

Alarm. Wake up. Dress. Commute. Coffee. Work. Leave. Eat. Sleep.

Alarm. Wake up. Dress. Commute. Coffee. Work. Leave. Eat. Sleep.

Wake up. Dress. Coffee. Work. Eat. Sleep.

Alarm. Snooze. Alarm. Wake up. Dress. Commute. Coffee. Work. Leave. Eat. Sleep.

Alarm. Wake up. Dress. Commute. Coffee. Work. Leave. Eat. Sleep.

Wake up. Eat. Sleep.

Wake up. Eat. Sleep.

_repeat_

{% </poem> %}

Every day the same as the last. Always running, never moving.

I tie my shoes.

I'm not sure when it started, and I worry that it might never end. Even the illusion of novelty feels overused now;
every day boasts "the new thing", "the life changer", "what you have been missing all this time", and I feel ashamed
from my own gulability. An endless stream of colors and words, trinkets and gimmicks, trends and in the end, distilled
into a single, silent, scream:

{% <poem> %}

_not enough_

{% </poem> %}

I put on my coat.

Whether directed to the subject or object is of little consequence. Grant every vice and every lust, gorged on attraction
and allure, yet the fattened hand stretches out for more. Rage against consumerism, tear down the source of abundance to
free those trapped by greed, only to become ensnared yourself. Turn your view inward to better yourself and live your
best life, and see yourself either succumb to the best of your vices or become lost in the sea of self-defined
purposelessness. Embrace the nihilism, tell yourself that nothing matters, and see where you've ended up. Is that truly
what you think is "enough"?

Justice? From me?
